A character at the end of a line, signalling: this text was written with AI support.

When a comment, ticket or message ends in ※, an AI phrased the text or contributed to its substance. Clicking the character leads here and shows which model was involved and what it contributed.

Approval is the endorsement: whoever releases a text — by hitting “send”, or by reading an agent’s draft and giving the go — answers for the content, exactly as with text they typed themselves. Only the exception is marked separately — if a contribution went out without anyone approving it, this page says so.

What the mark carries is never sent to a server — it sits in the link itself. This page is static: no cookies, nothing loaded, nothing measured.

To use it yourself, give your agent the Colophon skill — it appends the mark on its own.
